Hotel schema builder

Structured data (JSON-LD) for a hotel's own website, with every option a hotel can fill in: address, check-in and check-out times, the languages your staff speak, amenities, room types and your direct booking page. The essentials take about two minutes; open any other section to add more. Copy the code into the page's <head>.

What it leaves out, on purpose

A star rating goes in only with the body that awarded it. It never writes reviews or an average rating the hotel gives itself: Google doesn't show review stars that a business publishes about itself. It doesn't mark prices as available either, because that needs live availability behind it. Everything in the markup should also be visible on the page you paste it into.

Runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your draft stays in this browser until you press "Start again".
