Why AI matters

Visitors ask an AI before they book, and again when they arrive

Travellers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and similar assistants where to stay, what to see and where to eat, both while planning and while on the trip. If the assistant does not know your business, or gets it wrong, that visitor goes elsewhere. We optimise for these assistants as well as for Google.

74%of US travellers have used AI while planning a trip, to find hotels, routes and transport
49%use AI during the journey itself, for real-time information and local recommendations
~80%of regular AI users in Japan who travel have used it on trips to find local food and routes

Sources: Amadeus, Tailored Horizons (August 2026, US travellers; the first two figures, and 55% say local guidance on arrival is the AI feature they want most). JTB Tourism Research & Consulting, Survey on the Use of Generative AI and Travel (July 2025, 432 Japanese travellers who use generative AI weekly). Phocuswright found nearly 40% of US travellers used generative AI to plan trips in 2025, up 11 points in a year. These are surveys of US and Japanese travellers, not of Prague visitors. We do not have a Prague-specific figure.
