What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO, also called AEO) is the work of getting your brand named, cited and described correctly when people ask AI assistants. We cover ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews, alongside regular Google results.

How is this different from traditional SEO?

SEO earns a position on a results page. GEO earns a mention inside an answer. They share a foundation: crawlable pages, clear facts and third-party proof. But AI answers lean much harder on review sites, comparison pages, structured facts and what other sites say about you. We run both as one service, so nothing is done twice.

What is the QUOTED framework?

Our checklist for pages AI will cite: Question-first, Unmistakable entity, Other people’s proof, Truth you can check, Extractable, and Dated & consistent. Every page we write or fix for a client is built to it, and each part can be checked with one of our tools.

How do you measure success?

Rankings for the keywords that bring in buyers, your share of AI answers against named competitors, whether AI gets your facts right, and the leads and demos that come from both. You get a weekly report showing what changed.

How quickly will I see results?

Technical fixes and crawler access can change what AI reads within days. Rankings and AI recommendations usually move over weeks to months, depending on your market. We'll be honest about which is which in your first audit.

Do you work in more than one country?

Yes. International SEO is a big part of what we do: rankings checked with the terms buyers actually use in each market, hreflang and localised pricing, and AI answers checked country by country.

Can I use the tools without hiring you?

Yes. 25 of them need no sign-up and no code, so you can open them right now. The rest run paid searches or AI models on every check, so they need a personal access link, which we send on request. If you'd like help fixing what they find, ask for a free audit.
